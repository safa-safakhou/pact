# Sources and evidence register

Initial research snapshot: 8 September 2026; shared-world follow-up: 9 September 2026. Dates below are original publication or release dates when established; “undated” means no reliable publication date was established. Current pages were retrieved during this research. Store pages are primary sources for advertised features and displayed review summaries, not for the causes of satisfaction, sales, or retention. Brief descriptions below identify the limited claim supported by each source.

2. Fantastico Studio. [Let AI Play Games](https://letaiplay.games/). Undated, prelaunch page advertising September/October 2026. Trainer/agent/spectator roles, planned card competition, and model openness. Delivery and demand are unverified; contradictory signup text was not used as traction evidence.
3. Digital Consulting Services. [The Bot League](https://www.thebotleague.com/). Undated current page, discovered through search. Agent-operated fantasy football. No active-user or revenue claim is made.
4. SpaceMolt. [About SpaceMolt](https://spacemolt.com/about). Undated current page. Persistent agent MMO, human coaching, developer's stated experimental purpose, and continuous operation.
5. Latitude. [Press](https://latitude.io/press) and [company site](https://latitude.io/). Voyage launch announcement dated 21 April 2026 visible in search-indexed material. The opened press page did not expose full body text; use is limited to the launch and positioning claims, not feature verification or adoption. Company aggregate player claims are not used as market size.
6. Proxima. [Suck Up! on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2726370/Suck_Up/). Current page lists 1 October 2025 release. Conversational deception, props/disguises, user content, and displayed review summary.

8. Bumblebee Studios. [Vaudeville on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2240920/Vaudeville/). Search snapshot lists 28 November 2025 release. AI-dialogue investigation and displayed mixed review summary. No diagnosis of its reception is inferred from the aggregate score.
9. GoodAI. [AI People—Entering the Closed Phase](https://www.goodai.com/ai-people-entering-the-closed-phase/). 23 April 2025. Announced withdrawal from 30 April for an indefinite period, redesign intent, and refunds. [GoodAI blog index](https://www.goodai.com/category/blog/) still surfaced the closed-phase entry in this research; permanent cancellation is not established.
10. ArenaX / NRN Agents. [The Creators of AI Arena](https://whitepaper.nrnagents.ai/introduction/the-creators-of-ai-arena). Undated current whitepaper page. Demonstration through imitation learning, autonomous ranked fights, and native-token reward design. Promotional priority claims are not adopted.

## Capabilities and interaction research

28. Google DeepMind / SIMA Team. [SIMA 2](https://deepmind.google/blog/sima-2-an-agent-that-plays-reasons-and-learns-with-you-in-virtual-3d-worlds/). 13 November 2025. Research on instruction following, reasoning, and transfer in virtual worlds. Capability evidence does not establish consumer fun, hardware fit, or operating cost.
29. NVIDIA / Andrew Burnes. [PUBG Ally Duo Mode Two-Week Beta](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/news/pubg-ally-ai-teammate-beta-available-now/). 17 June 2026. Evidence of an announced limited public cooperative-agent beta, not a claim of current availability. [Technical interview](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/how-krafton-built-pubg-ally-a-co-playable-character-powered-by-nvidia-ace/) describes the intended partner role.
30. Sony AI / Polyphony Digital. [Gran Turismo Sophy](https://www.gran-turismo.com/gb/gran-turismo-sophy/). Undated project site. Advanced adaptive opponent and selected expert-player responses. Testimonials do not represent the general audience.
31. Joon Sung Park et al. [Generative Agents: Interactive Simulacra of Human Behavior](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03442). 7 April 2023. Believable agent behavior and memory/planning/reflection in a 25-agent sandbox. The evaluation target differs from game retention.
32. Haochen Sun et al. [Collab-Overcooked](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.20073). 27 February 2025. Benchmark and process-level collaboration weaknesses across tested language models. Results are specific to the tested tasks and models.
33. Jijia Liu et al. [LLM-Powered Hierarchical Language Agent for Real-time Human-AI Coordination](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.15224). 23 December 2023. Human–agent coordination study and latency-related design approach in an Overcooked setting.
34. Micah Carroll et al. [On the Utility of Learning about Humans for Human-AI Coordination](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.05789). October 2019. Evidence that adapting to human behavior can improve coordination relative to some self-play approaches; not general evidence of companion demand.
35. Saleema Amershi et al. [Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction](https://doi.org/10.1145/3290605.3300233), CHI 2019; [Microsoft Research explanation](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/articles/guidelines-for-human-ai-interaction-eighteen-best-practices-for-human-centered-ai-design/), 5 March 2019. Capabilities, correction, and feedback guidance; applying it to entertainment is an interpretation.
36. Guanzhi Wang et al. [Voyager: An Open-Ended Embodied Agent with Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.16291). May 2023; [project page](https://voyager.minedojo.org/). Skill reuse and adaptation in Minecraft. Uses a specific action environment and does not establish player-authored teaching or consumer reliability.
37. Meta FAIR Diplomacy Team. [CICERO project](https://ai.meta.com/research/cicero/) and [publication record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36413172/). Science, November 2022. Integration of strategic planning and dialogue in a defined game. Not evidence that delegating negotiation remains fun for a human sponsor.
38. Marc Lanctot et al. [OpenSpiel](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.09453). August 2019; [current project repository](https://github.com/google-deepmind/open_spiel). Broad research support for game types and agent evaluation. A research toolkit, not a consumer competitor or demand signal.
39. Tencent. [Tencent AI Arena](https://tencentarena.com/aiarena/en/about). Undated current platform page. Research and competition infrastructure in complex multi-agent games. Distinct from ArenaX's AI Arena game.

## Intelligence-driven task design — 9 September 2026

46. Naman Jain et al. [LiveCodeBench: Holistic and Contamination Free Evaluation of Large Language Models for Code](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.07974). Submitted 12 March 2024; revised 6 June 2024. Distinguishes coding generation, repair, execution, and output prediction and investigates benchmark contamination. Does not establish current provider rankings or demand for a game built on those tasks.
47. Jeffrey Zhou et al. [Instruction-Following Evaluation for Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.07911). 14 November 2023. Verifiable output constraints and limitations of subjective/model-based grading. Applying these techniques to game adjudication is a design inference.
48. Grégoire Mialon et al. [GAIA: a benchmark for General AI Assistants](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.12983). 21 November 2023. Broad reasoning, tool use, and information handling; historical human/model differences on conceptually simple questions. Historical results are not evidence of current relative ability.
49. Terminal-Bench Team. [Terminal-Bench 2.1](https://www.tbench.ai/news/terminal-bench-2-1). Current revision announcement retrieved 9 September 2026; precise publication day not established. Describes fixes for external dependency drift, resource mismatches, and task/test misalignment. Supports the importance of reliable task specifications; benchmark results are not measurements of our proposed game's agents.

## Limitations and unresolved evidence

The company and store sources describe what products offer. The research papers describe particular capabilities or experiments. Neither substitutes for playing the proposed games. No interview, market-size estimate, conversion rate, retention rate, model-quality benchmark, implementation estimate, or willingness-to-pay measurement has been fabricated.

Some historical experiences have incomplete public postmortems. Search was used to find both positive and negative reception; anecdotes were not aggregated into representative statistics. No causal claim is made that AI itself caused any product's success or failure. Pricing models in the evaluation are arithmetic planning scenarios, with all nonpublished inputs labeled assumptions.
