# TITAN

**Historical proposal:** the founder rejected this direction in favor of settlements whose agents freely communicate, negotiate, and act in a shared world. [AFTER THE CROWN](/Users/safa.safakhou/repos/mine/random_idea/product-discovery/20-after-the-crown.md) develops that later direction. The combat-machine loop below is not the current design.

**Build a machine with your AI. Give it a fighting identity. See what it can become.**

You start with a small mechanical creature in a shared world of hostile machines and other players. Your AI is its brain. Together you develop it into something distinctive: a cunning hunter, a deceptive infiltrator, or a walking fortress.

You choose what you want it to become. Your agent investigates enemies, develops tactics, and makes your available parts work together. Tell it, “I want something that tricks stronger opponents,” and it might develop a decoy opening that draws an enemy's counter before revealing its main weapon. The game tests whether that behavior actually works.

**Hunt → bring home new parts → evolve → challenge something stronger.**

A successful hunt can unlock an ability that changes your next build. You choose whether to bring valuable loot home or risk it on another encounter. Defeat costs the expedition's unsecured haul; your core, unlocked designs, and history remain. The next attempt starts with a specific ambition: a new tactic, a new part, or a rival you want to beat.

Hunt with friends to combine your Titans' abilities and split the haul, or go alone and keep the rewards. Challenge rival creations, trade advice, build an alliance, and earn levels, trophies, and cosmetics. Seasons bring new enemies and competitive honors while your Titan and collection persist.

The game lives in chat and simple status cards, with short, factual combat replays. When your LLM disconnects, accepted behaviors can still execute, but fresh investigation and adaptation wait until it is available again.

The intended feeling is: **“That is my Titan. We built that trick. Now I want to see whether it can beat yours.”**

This is the recommended direction to test, not an implemented or validated game. The 2,000-mission completion race is retired as the core. [The research and comparison of alternatives](/Users/safa.safakhou/repos/mine/random_idea/product-discovery/18-agent-game-research-reset.md) explain the choice and its main risks.
