# PACT-165 — inactive rare-finish review

Only pure offline arithmetic/enumeration is prepared. The [artifact](assets/rare-finish-review-20261010.json) is reproducible with `rareFinishReview()` in `scripts/evaluation/rare-finishes.ts`. Enumerating every integer 0–9999 gives the candidate distribution 7500 Common / 2000 Uncommon / 400 Rare / 100 Mythic. After 29 eligible misses, every next draw is Mythic and resets the candidate miss count; a natural Mythic also resets it. Invalid draws/counts are rejected. The meaningful unit case is included in the 356-test unit run.

Protection changes the distribution: expected draws per Mythic are exactly the rational geometric sum in the artifact, approximately **26.02996**, giving **3.84173% long-run Mythic**, rather than a flat 1%. About **74.71721%** of fresh cycles reach the protected thirtieth draw. An ordinary next draw would be 1%; the protected next draw would be 100%. Draw counts are not days: earned eligibility frequency and human waiting experience are unmeasured.

**Activation remains blocked.** There is no runtime import, RNG mint, issuance ledger, enrollment, public odds promise, paid reroll, combat/economy change or model call. A reviewed activation must settle exact account/source/version uniqueness, game-week/opponent eligibility and event ordering before durable allocation; retries, rollback, restart and season boundaries must preserve protection rather than reroll. Synthetic cohorts stay excluded unless separately enrolled. Actual issuance/exploit rates, long-wait impact and human comprehension need separately bounded consenting observation. Pure enumeration does not satisfy these live acceptance items. Deterministic campaign keepsakes remain available independently.
