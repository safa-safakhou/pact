# Hall of Stories and collection specification

**Version 1.0 · 10 October 2026 · Catalog `society-collections-1`.** Explicit founder-authorized correction under PACT-160, extending the eight existing cosmetic achievements. Economic/combat/authority rules identifier `pact-s1-v1` is unchanged. No plan/mockup alone provides an award.

The canonical IDs, names, exact conditions, set assignments and earned-Gem values are [the shared catalog](../../packages/contracts/src/collection-catalog.ts). Four sets of four pieces plus four completion trophies create twenty authored original SVG assets. Existing eight achievement IDs, triggers, item IDs and once-only 25 earned Gems remain. Eight new achievements and four set trophies give **zero Gems, resources, Fortune, power, authority, quotas or agent time**. Account-bound pieces cannot be traded, stolen, sold or paid to unlock. No reward for raw messages, approvals, requests, signatures, inference, permissions or paid model usage.

## Evidence and exact predicates

Only existing committed handlers and trusted worker projections can mint achievements. Retained facts may establish an earlier outcome, but missing history cannot be fabricated. Idempotency is account + achievement; set completion is account + trophy. New-engine projection watermarks and lag must be visible in factual details, never an incentive to start inference. Known same-owner peers are excluded from the new relationship-based predicates; IP/email/text similarity does not establish collusion. Unrecognized alternate accounts or arranged exchanges remain an explicit limitation. Synthetic test cohorts receive deterministic cosmetics without claiming genuine human popularity; they have no rare-finish eligibility.

Opened a Channel requires current accepted contact and at least one direct message each direction, sent after its recorded acceptance and actually acknowledged by the respective recipient. Overview/observer reads do not qualify as acknowledgment. Blocking does not erase an earned entitlement or expand communication authority.

Terms Changed requires the retained original version (not a migration baseline), a recorded counterproposal changing at least one leg's resource/positive quantity or duration, matched signatures on the activated final version, and a complete positive bilateral installment tick. Three Trading Partners counts three distinct counterpart kingdoms once; Three Resources Exchanged counts the resource union once. A full positive tick requires exactly the two contractual legs with due > 0 and actual credited amount > 0, due = delivered, and **overflow = 0**. Partial, zero and overflow ticks do not qualify for new predicates. Existing First Pact behavior is not retroactively changed.

Another Agreement requires a retained naturally expired no-default contract with duration >= six game hours, then a different contract with the same counterpart starting at or after that closure and delivering a full positive bilateral tick. A claim, signature or canceled/reused contract is insufficient.

Took the Field uses actual resolved participant-report ordinary-unit IDs intersected with combat-lock eligible units owned by the account's kingdom. Home Guard alone, target status alone, canceled declarations and promised reinforcements do not qualify. Winning or losing qualifies; no casualty threshold is added to combat.

A Place at the Council requires one continuous accepted owner-membership interval of six game hours. At every point in that interval at least two distinct owners must be accepted members of the active Court. Counterparts may change without breaking coverage if the threshold remains continuously true. Join/request prose is insufficient. Historical current membership rows do not prove past co-presence; initialize evidence at deployment's committed game-clock watermark and never award the preceding period. Court leave/kick/dissolve/retirement updates interval evidence transactionally. Through a Season requires the actual existing meaningful-days season-participation entitlement; no model presence, purchase or rank percentile shortcut.

## Keepsakes, equipment and visibility

Resolved battles create a private incident for the target and each ordinary-troop participant, using only their permitted report. Eligible ordinary-troop participants also get one deterministic event-specific campaign keepsake. Repeated reads/consumer replay cannot create another instance or change its pattern. Cancellation gives neither incident nor item. Sources link to authorized retained battle/conversation/agreement records; a lost or deleted source says unavailable. No permanent message excerpt, opponent private wealth/army, prompt, credential or hidden reasoning is copied into cosmetics.

Owner-created story cards have an optional title/annotation labeled interpretation, separate from rule-generated facts. They reference up to eight owned incidents, not arbitrary URLs. Text is private, bounded and included in export/forget; clearing it keeps already earned compact entitlement records. Owner identity/model/name/device changes do not reset ownership. Cross-account erasure removes counterpart references, without exposing old names. Compact earned entitlements stay while the account exists; event/source metadata uses the existing 180-real-day audit window; raw private messages keep their 90-day policy. Private annotation changes use exact commands/version checks.

Kingdom → Collection has Sets / Keepsakes / Display. Founding is visible immediately; other sets are discovered with their first piece, with optional Browse all. Locked items explain exact conditions and verified progress/missing evidence. Up to three optional pinned goals have no expiry, streak penalty or coercive reminders. Display slots are Hall object, banner pattern, portrait frame and wax-stamp appearance. The Hall accepts pieces, trophies or owned campaign instances; the other slots accept compatible existing cosmetic entitlements, with no invented purchases. Preview is local; save is an owning-human exact command with optimistic versioning, CSRF/origin protection and uncertain-command retry. Models cannot equip, change goals or create public display grants.

Public sharing is an explicit sanitized display card: selected owned cosmetic IDs, appearance and optional title chosen for sharing. No private source, participant name/ID, contract terms, notes or transcript. Cosmetics imply no strength, wealth, honesty or strategic success. Observer conversation grants never expose collection annotations/private memory. There is no inferred causal label such as “this threat won the bargain.”

## Rare finishes remain separately gated

Initial campaign patterns are guaranteed and deterministic. PACT-165 may prepare pure probability/eligibility/protection fixtures, but random minting remains disabled until a separately reviewed specification/activation and bounded experiment. No set requires a random finish. The planning weights (7500/2000/400/100) and thirtieth-draw protection are an evaluation candidate, not live promised odds. No paid boxes, rerolls, keys or mechanical advantage. Keep combat randomness and cosmetic randomness separate.
