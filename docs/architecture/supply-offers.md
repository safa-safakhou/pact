# Supply offers, signatures, bonds, and activation

`@pact/kingdom` owns migration `0025_supply_offers.sql`, `SupplyService`, and the pure `SupplyTerms` validator. A typed offer has one optional hourly whole-resource flow and one optional whole-resource bond from each side, a 1/6/24/72-hour duration, a 0/1/6-hour notice no longer than the duration, an exact version, and 5–60 game-minute expiry (15 by default). Both flows cannot be zero. Rates must be positive and no greater than the payer's current per-resource storage cap per hour at signing. A bond needs an outgoing flow and cannot exceed that side's marked six-hour-or-remaining flow value. Self-contracts and blocked pairs fail. No contact relationship or gift implies a signature.

Create/counter/sign commands consume the kingdom's shared offer-action rolling allowance only when their transaction commits. A counteroffer increments the version, making earlier signatures stale and releasing their provisional agent authority. `SupplyExposureAdapter` now reserves each agent signer's full future outgoing flow plus bond, releases it on counter, withdrawal, or expiry, charges the posted bond at activation, consumes actual installments, and releases the remainder on closure. Approved human exceptions use a separate exact-permit commitment, not the agent allowance. Human signatures are valid only after the authenticated human command/permit path has admitted them; this internal service is not a public endpoint.

The second exact-version signature activates at most one contract in the same transaction. It rechecks rate caps, eight outgoing/eight incoming active slots per kingdom, 10% total locked-bond capacity, and both parties' ordinary bond funds. All bond locks, the contract row, authority transition, offer status, and outbox event commit or roll back together. A replay of a final signature returns the existing contract. Start is the strictly next game-minute boundary; end is start plus the selected duration. `supply_contracts` retains exact terms for PACT-038's minute delivery/default settlement. Outbox events contain IDs and timings, not private terms.

The rules worker expires unsigned offers at their game deadline and releases provisional authority. Withdrawal and expiry remain available even if parties later block each other; blocking stops only new commitments. Closed unactivated offers are retained 180 real days; account export includes participant offers/contracts. Run `npm run db:migrate` before restarting the rules worker on the single VPS. Tests use PostgreSQL 17 synthetic schemas; no hosted deployment or live player validation is claimed.

## Minute settlement and closure

Migration `0026_supply_settlement.sql` records each directional installment, including due/delivered internal units, recipient overflow, and mechanical failure reason. The rules worker first closes contracts whose end, valid notice, mutual release, or explicit refusal is due at the boundary. After production and upkeep, `SupplySettlementService.capture` reads each payer's ordinary balance, remaining Grain allowance, and current human mandate reserve floor through a transaction-bound authority port. Mandate floor strings are exact internal units (1/600 resource); absent floors are zero. Grain may count allowance toward its protected floor, but exports debit ordinary stock only. All obligations from one payer/resource share the captured available amount proportionally; leftover internal units go by contract ID.

`settleDeliveries` debits every outgoing allocation before crediting any incoming allocation, so circular promises do not fund one another in a tick. The two directions remain independent. `settleDefaults` then closes every underfunded contract without reversing the funded counterparty's delivery. Natural expiry and notice/mutual release return bonds; a defaulting side forfeits its bond to the other side, while a nondefaulting side gets its own bond back. Simultaneous defaults exchange both bonds. Bond returns and compensation obey recipient storage caps and record overflow through the ledger. The default outbox event contains only contract/party IDs and game time; the private installment and close records retain amounts and causes.

Internal human commands call `giveNotice`, `signRelease`, or `refuse` inside their authenticated transaction; these methods derive party membership from account ownership. Notice of zero hours and mutual release become effective at the strictly next minute boundary. A refusal also defaults at the next boundary. These are not yet public HTTP/client controls. Account export includes participating contracts and their directional installments; retention removes closed contracts (and cascading installments and offers) 180 real days after closure. The authority adapter preserves the no-escrow resource-settlement order.
