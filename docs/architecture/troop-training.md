# Troop identity, training, and placement

`@pact/kingdom` owns the immutable UUIDv7 `kingdom_troops` identities introduced at founding and migration `0021_troop_training.sql`. `TroopService` is transaction-bound; no client or provider adapter may mint, copy, or reassign units independently. The service has no HTTP/MCP route yet. Later raid, scout, and garrison handlers call its selection/commitment methods after their own action-specific authorization and terms checks.

One active training batch of 1–40 units is allowed per kingdom. Admission checks the owner, Hall unlock, Barracks owned capacity including all living owned units (even those lent elsewhere), and one-queue constraint under a kingdom row lock. It pays the full Iron/Silver price from **ordinary stock only** and records separate training cost provenance. Each unit completes sequentially at its exact game-minute deadline, obtains a new UUIDv7 identity, and begins upkeep only then. Training does not accelerate when Barracks changes. Canceling an incomplete batch refunds 80% of only the uncompleted ordinary cost, rounded down in internal units. A completed unit and its paid cost never become part of a remaining-batch refund. Duplicate queue/cancel/tick calls cannot duplicate units or value.

`assign` binds lowest eligible immutable IDs of a requested ordinary type, or validates an explicit visible ID set, while holding the owner/host kingdom rows. It excludes Home Guard, non-home units, and battle-locked IDs. A troop has exactly one placement (`home`, `visiting`, `scouting`, or `attacking`) and one placement reference; a second exclusive assignment cannot reuse it. Visiting assignments check the lender's Hall III unlock and the host's separate Barracks visitor pool. Owned capacity continues counting lent units. `returnPlacement` is an internal completion/contract-release port and must not be exposed as a player cancellation of a scout. Home Guard stays at home, can defend, and can be disbanded outside a battle lock for zero refund; trained units may also be disbanded for zero. A disband deletes the living identity and immediately removes its upkeep obligation.

`captureBattleEligibility` stores a many-to-many snapshot of existing troop IDs, not a second troop inventory. It is idempotent by battle ID and preserves attacker/defender sides. A defender may appear in multiple overlapping battle snapshots at its one location; an individual ID cannot be on both sides of the same snapshot. Active locks block disband, return, and reassignment until the specified game time. Later combat tickets own casualty resolution and retained outcome reporting.

The rules worker settles due training after building completion and before production/upkeep in the same atomic world tick. On the single VPS, apply `0021_troop_training.sql` before starting the updated rules worker. Tests use isolated PostgreSQL 17 schemas and synthetic owners/units. No live gameplay, hosted CI, or deployment validation is claimed.
