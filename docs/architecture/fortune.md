# Fortune accounting and provisional standings

`@pact/kingdom` exports `FortuneService`. Wealth is **derived from committed source rows**, not incremented by game events. The exact unit is one quarter of a 1/600 resource: ordinary Iron ×4, Silver ×8, Grain ×1. Divide `wealthQuarterUnits` and `fortuneQuarterUnits` by 2,400 for display Fortune. Serialized API-facing values are decimal strings; negative Fortune is valid.

`wealth(client, kingdomId)` counts ordinary plus still-owned locked bonds in resource balances; ordinary remaining cost provenance for active construction/training; original ordinary paid construction value once installed; and surviving owned non-Home-Guard troops at the fixed catalog recruitment cost. A visiting unit remains the lender's wealth until death or seizure. It excludes founding allowances, allowance-funded construction, future contract receipts, Home Guard, and cosmetics. Completed training costs are no longer counted because surviving troop identities replace them. Canceled costs are excluded after the ledger posts the refund/loss. Bond forfeiture and recipient credit, transfers, loot, and seizure therefore change ownership once in their existing atomic command transactions.

`provisional(client, gameTimeMs, band?, limit?)` computes live `W − W₀` from the same snapshot. It exposes handle, Hall band, rank, signed score, baseline, and an explicit `provisional: true`; it does not expose another player's stock or cost breakdown. Hall can only rise in this ruleset, so current level is the highest reached. The overall board and I–II, III–IV, V–VI filters use competition ranks. This method must be called inside a read-only, consistent transaction by the eventual API adapter, with authenticated read-rate limits.

At an exact game-hour boundary, the rules worker writes one `fortune_hourly_snapshots` row per ranked kingdom in the final tick phase, after combat and deliveries. `(scope, season, kingdom, hour)` is unique; replays insert nothing. Migration `0032_fortune.sql` owns these snapshots and its ranking index. The existing `kingdom_score_baselines` table stores W₀. The test-world season key is `prototype`; PACT-069 must configure the season clock and reset W₀ from the pre-boundary committed wealth at season rollover. No final seasonal prizes or eligible-player certification are awarded here.

Operationally, apply `0032_fortune.sql` before starting the updated rules worker. A missing hour is evidence of a paused/failed world and must not be filled by wall-clock catch-up; repair through the world-clock recovery process. Stored snapshots are historical scoring samples, while live owner score is recalculated from current state.
