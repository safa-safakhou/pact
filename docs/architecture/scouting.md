# Timed scouting and private reports

`ScoutService` in `@pact/kingdom` starts and completes a scout inside the caller's gameplay transaction. Migration `0030_scout_reports.sql` owns timed operations and explicit report shares. The rules worker calls `finishDue` in `battle_and_scouts`, after the battle-resolution hook; PACT-044 connects the final combat resolver ahead of this call.

Admission derives the player's ranked kingdom from the authenticated account, rejects self/unknown targets, and binds the two lowest eligible ordinary home Spearmen (or two explicit permitted UUIDs). Home Guard, already-placed, and battle-locked units cannot leave. The action atomically spends one scout charge, 10 ordinary Silver, and 5 ordinary Grain; restricted allowances never pay. An agent must also reserve a two-unit commitment and enough Silver/Grain authority under `scout.start`. Its resource exposure is consumed on admission, while the troop commitment remains until completion. The unique per-kingdom operation key returns the existing timed order on retry without charging again. A target-notice outbox event identifies the scout kingdom.

The deadline is the strictly next minute boundary plus ten game minutes. Completion samples the committed state at that tick: target home and visiting troop counts by type, exact Wall level, and ordinary stored resource bands at 1/600 precision. Scouts and attacking troops elsewhere, visiting-unit owners, suppliers, private contracts, locked bonds, and founding allowances are absent. Reports carry the game timestamp and a stale-after-observation marker. `readReport` allows only the scout's account or a kingdom explicitly added through `shareReport`; raw operation rows are not a public read surface. Completed reports and shares are included in account export and purged after 180 real days.

PACT-049 will expose authenticated commands and authorized projections; clients must not call the rules service directly or reconstruct a finer report. PACT-044 must resolve same-boundary battles before `finishDue`; until then local tests verify the phase placement and post-change sampling, not a real overlapping-battle result.
