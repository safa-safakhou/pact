# 14 Homeostatic Reinforcement Learning for Integrating Reward Collection and Physiological Stability

[Keramati and Gutkin, eLife, 2014](https://elifesciences.org/articles/04811). Coverage: main text and mathematical Methods, PDF pages 1–23; separate figure supplements and simulation parameter files not audited. Proofs read, not independently rederived.

**Mechanism.** Define reward through reduction in internal deviation from desired states. Learning uses both internal and external state, allowing anticipatory behavior rather than only immediate correction.

**Evidence.** Conditional mathematical equivalence between discounted reward and drive objectives, plus small simulations explaining prior conditioning findings, risk sensitivity and competing needs. No game evaluation or new animal experiment.

**Limits.** The formal setup assumes full observability, fixed setpoints and a particular drive function. The proof considers trajectories ending at the setpoint. General social and novelty rewards remain outside scope. Biased outcome estimates or extra hedonic reward can defeat regulation. Finite simulation confinement is not a general safety guarantee.

**Mission implication — inference.** Persistent game needs could influence priorities and learning, but survival and competent play need not have identical objectives.

**Test suggested.** Compare explicit constraints, state-dependent rewards and conventional task rewards; measure survival, task progress and proxy exploitation separately.
