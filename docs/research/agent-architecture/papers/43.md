## 43 — Mastering Diverse Domains through World Models (DreamerV3)

**Source/coverage:** Hafner et al., [arXiv v2, April 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.04104v2), main pp. 1–11, methods and supplementary text/tables pp. 17–40 read; references partly read. Figure curves not visually audited.

**Mechanism:** Recurrent world model, imagined actor–critic training and fast policy execution. Balanced losses, categorical uncertainty, transformed prediction targets and return normalization stabilize learning across domains.

**Evidence:** Over 150 tasks spanning eight domains, with shared core hyperparameters and multiple seeds in most benchmarks. Minecraft agents all discover diamonds during training; size/replay ablations show gains from more computation.

**Limits/contrary evidence:** Generality concerns the learning algorithm: agents are trained separately. Model size, replay ratio and environment counts vary by benchmark. Minecraft uses inventory/milestone/health vectors, abstract crafting and modified input mechanics, plus intermediate milestone rewards. After 100 million steps, diamonds occur in only 0.4% of episodes; every training run discovering one is a different metric. Exploration remains weak on BSuite Deep Sea. ProcGen uses one seed. Comparisons mix budgets and protocols.

**Mission implication — inference:** Treat this as a serious general learning baseline, without assuming hierarchy is necessary. Measure competence per episode, observation/action privileges, adaptation and total training resources; discovery at least once is insufficient evidence of reliable play.
