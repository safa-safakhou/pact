## 37 — DeepMimic: Example-Guided Deep Reinforcement Learning of Physics-Based Character Skills

**Source/coverage:** Peng et al., [2018 author paper](https://xbpeng.github.io/projects/DeepMimic/DeepMimic_2018.pdf). Complete main text and embedded supplement read; plots/images not independently audited; videos not reviewed.

**Mechanism:** Train feedback policies with motion-imitation and task rewards. Policies output joint targets to PD servos. Reference-state initialization and failure termination structure learning. Several methods combine or select skills, including feasibility estimates from independently learned value functions.

**Evidence:** Simulated human, robot, dinosaur, and dragon motions, task variants, perturbation tests and ablations. Policies run at 30 Hz; physics runs faster. Main statistics for most skills come from one training run. Training typically requires tens of millions of samples per skill.

**Limits/contrary evidence:** New morphology requires retraining; directly transferring human policies to Atlas fails. Phase synchronization, servo gains, reward similarity and motion retargeting require design. Small skill sets are demonstrated. Task-only training can score well through unintended behavior: carrying a ball toward the target instead of throwing it. Appearance and task success are distinct objectives.

**Mission implication — inference:** Mechanical competence can be pretrained from examples and combined with task optimization, but it depends on the actuator interface. Separate desired movement style, legal task completion, recovery, and strategic competence in evaluation; none substitutes for the others.
