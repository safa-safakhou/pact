## 62 — Recovery RL: Safe Reinforcement Learning with Learned Recovery Zones

**Source/coverage:** Thananjeyan et al., [arXiv v2, May 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.15920v2). Read main text and supplementary prose/tables throughout 15-page PDF; figures/code not independently audited.

**Mechanism:** Learn a task policy, recovery policy and risk critic. Offline examples of constraint violations initialize recovery knowledge; a risk threshold triggers takeover. Task-learning transitions credit proposed actions with the resulting protected outcome.

**Evidence:** Six simulation tasks and physical image-based obstacle avoidance; ten simulation seeds and three physical runs. Reported success/violation ratios beat five adapted baselines. Ablations show offline pretraining and action relabeling matter substantially.

**Limits/contrary evidence:** This is learned risk management, not a demonstrated hard safety guarantee. Model-free recovery performs poorly on manipulation compared with model-based recovery. Failures can occur during recovery; that does not isolate the cause, because late detection is counted as recovery failure. Physical visual-task failures chiefly implicate risk estimation. Data deliberately includes collisions; manipulation also uses successful demonstrations. Reward plots exclude violating episodes and must be read conditionally.

**Mission implication — inference:** Separate protection can improve learning without an exact model, but the detector and recovery behavior both need evaluation. Measure failure counts, successful play and total data cost separately rather than relying only on their ratio.
