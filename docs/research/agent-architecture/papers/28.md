## 28 — The Option-Critic Architecture

**Source/coverage:** Bacon, Harb and Precup, [arXiv v2, December 2016](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.05140v2), AAAI 2017. All nine pages read, including derivations; proofs not independently verified.

**Mechanism:** Jointly learn option policies, termination rules, and selection from task reward. Differentiable options share a learned representation; a critic estimates values. No supplied semantic subgoals or intrinsic task rewards are necessary. The number of options is chosen beforehand.

**Evidence:** Four-room goal relocation, continuous-state Pinball, and four Atari games. Reported Atari results exceed the original 2013 DQN comparator in three games. This is a historical comparator, not evidence against all flat learners. Separate training occurs per game.

**Limits/contrary evidence:** Options tend to shrink toward primitive actions when optimizing return; termination regularization encourages persistence. Entropy regularization and a gradient baseline materially affect results. All options are assumed available everywhere. Practical discounted-gradient estimates are biased, deliberately trading theoretical purity against sample efficiency. Demonstrated transfer concerns changed goals within the navigation domain, not entry into arbitrary unseen games.

**Mission implication — inference:** Functional specialization can be learned jointly, without separately authored “brains.” Compare learned options against matched flat learners and supplied skills. Test actual persistence, diversity, transfer, switching overhead, and whether human-readable interpretations predict reliable behavior.
