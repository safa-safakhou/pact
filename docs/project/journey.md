## What the experiments taught us

There is evidence that the game supports development, communication, contracts and material delivery. The strongest early social results included extensive scripted counterparties and a separately labelled simulated-human contact controller. Later weak social results cannot simply be attributed to a missing communication reward; source/context, actual peer availability, legal targets, model limits and retrieval all mattered.

The founder's design direction preserved alternatives: loop engines beside event engines, optional grounded cognition beside basic reasoning, and an independent environment boundary. Survival and relationships should emerge from ordinary consequences and chosen responses, not a prescribed message/alliance count. No consciousness result or architecture winner has been demonstrated.

Latest [diagnostic findings](https://github.com/safa-safakhou/fleet/blob/master/docs/project/findings.md) distinguish valid structured output from correct reasoning, stored memory from delivered context, and a proposal from a receipt/outcome. The next useful milestone is one reliable complete cycle under exact source/model/environment conditions before larger populations.
